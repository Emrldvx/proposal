FAQ

Questions you might be asking

Why is design and frontend separate from the backend?

So you can start now. Brand, screens and the app's frontend don't depend on the licensing route or the crypto partner, which your PRD lists as still open. The backend quote is fixed once those are settled.

What does $3,500 – $6,000 for frontend development cover?

Building the mobile app for Android and iOS and the web admin dashboard from the approved designs, connected to the backend APIs and tested on real phones. Where it lands in the range depends on the final screen count confirmed in discovery.

Which technology will you build the app with?

One cross-platform codebase, React Native or Flutter. We recommend one in discovery based on performance on entry-level Android phones and who will maintain the app after launch.

What if we want changes after approving a design?

Each design phase includes 2 revision rounds. Changes after sign-off are billed hourly, and we tell you the cost before starting.

Who owns the designs and code?

You do. Figma files, brand assets and code sit in your accounts from day one, and the intellectual property transfers to you on payment.

What happens after launch?

A 60-day bug-fix warranty is included. After that, an optional maintenance retainer of $1,500 – $3,000 per month covers updates and fixes.

How do payments work?

Five payments, each tied to something you can see: kick-off, brand and UX approval, design handoff, screens working in staging, and the live app.

Anything else? Ask on the discovery call or reply to this proposal.
